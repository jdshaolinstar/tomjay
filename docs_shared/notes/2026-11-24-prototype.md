## Your part: the video prompt

- Open `/lab` on the tunnel link. Change the text, press Save, and the right side shows
  exactly what the video model receives.
- `style` is the look and has the biggest effect. `video` is the shot. `script` writes
  the three takes.
- Lab saves change files on Jermaine's machine. They are not in git until Jermaine
  commits them, so don't edit `prompts/` in git at the same time.
- "Copy video prompt" lets you test in any video tool. Upload the result with
  "Add a clip" on the take.
